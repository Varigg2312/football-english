Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Football English Academy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a language-learning application focused on football vocabulary and tactics. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Game progress (XP, rank, daily streak) — stored exclusively in your device's localStorage. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Username and password (if you register) — stored in localStorage for local session management. No server-side account database exists at this time.
  • Lesson interactions (quiz answers, search queries) — processed locally in your browser. Not stored or transmitted.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use cookies for tracking.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. AI Features (The Gaffer)

PRO users can interact with "The Gaffer", an AI tutor powered by a third-party large language model API. Messages you send to The Gaffer are transmitted to that API provider for processing. We recommend not sharing personal or sensitive information in chat. The API provider's privacy policy applies to those transmissions.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare Pages — hosts this web application. Cloudflare may log anonymous access metadata per their own privacy policy.
  • Stripe — processes PRO subscription payments. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data.
  • Font Awesome / Google Fonts — loaded from CDN. These providers may log your IP address per their policies.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing the TWA data removes all stored information permanently.

7. Your Rights

Since all data remains on your device and we hold no personal data on our servers, we currently have no user data to provide, modify, or delete upon request. If this changes in future versions, this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
